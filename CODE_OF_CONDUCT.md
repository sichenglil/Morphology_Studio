# Code of Conduct

Morphology Studio follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). Contributors must provide a harassment-free experience regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, color, religion, sexual identity and orientation, or technical choices.

Examples of acceptable behavior include empathy, constructive feedback, accountability, and respect for differing viewpoints. Unacceptable behavior includes sexualized language, trolling, insults, harassment, publishing private information, or conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

Maintainers may remove or reject contributions and temporarily or permanently ban participation. Report conduct incidents privately to the repository owner through GitHub's private contact mechanisms. The maintainer will acknowledge, assess conflicts of interest, investigate confidentially, communicate an outcome, and retain only information needed for enforcement. Enforcement follows the Contributor Covenant 2.1 ladder: correction, warning, temporary ban, then permanent ban.
